About CarmNote

CarmNote is a portable, reproducible, single-file computational notebook. The analysis engine, the visualisations, the interface, and your results all live inside one self-contained HTML file that runs entirely in your browser — no installation, no server, no runtime dependencies, and no data ever leaving your computer. Open it by double-clicking, work offline, and share the finished notebook as the same single file you started with.

This notebook is CarmNote SNA, for Social Network Analysis. CarmNote is part of the Carm ecosystem.

About Carm

Carm is a high-performance statistical computing and scientific computing platform created and maintained by Professor Mohammed Saqr, PhD. It is designed for advanced data analysis, statistical modeling, machine learning, and reproducible research, and is developed primarily for research and education while supporting commercial applications under a separate commercial license.
